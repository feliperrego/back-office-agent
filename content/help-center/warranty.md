We stand behind the gear we sell.

## What the warranty covers

Every item we sell is covered for 1 year against manufacturing defects, starting on its delivery date.

## What it doesn't cover

The warranty doesn't cover normal wear and tear, damage from accidents or misuse, or items that have been altered.

## Making a warranty claim

Contact our support team with your order number, a short description of the problem and a photo. A member of the team reviews the claim and, if it is covered, repairs or replaces the item.

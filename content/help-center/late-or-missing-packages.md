---
title: Late or missing packages
category: Shipping and delivery
---

Most orders arrive by their estimated delivery date. Here is what to do when one does not.

## My package is late

Check the tracking link first: carriers sometimes update it a day late. If your order is more than 2 business days past its estimated delivery date, contact our support team, and a member of the team will look into it with the carrier.

## Tracking says delivered, but I can't find it

Look around your door, your mailbox and your building's mail room, and ask your neighbors. Carriers sometimes mark a package as delivered shortly before it arrives, so wait until the end of the next business day. If it still has not turned up, contact our support team: a member of the team will open an investigation with the carrier.

category: Shipping and delivery

Acme Outfitters ships every order from our own warehouse, with the shipping option you choose at checkout.

## Standard shipping

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. It is free on orders over $75. On smaller orders, the shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Express shipping

Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $15, whatever the order total.

## How delivery times are counted

Delivery times count business days, Monday to Friday except US federal holidays, starting the day after your order ships.

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States only.

title: Damaged or defective items
category: Returns and refunds
---

We check every order before it ships, but things can still go wrong in transit or in use.

## Damaged on arrival

If an item arrives damaged, keep the packaging and contact our support team with your order number and a photo of the damage. A member of the team will arrange a replacement or a refund.

## Defective items

Inside the return window, you can send a defective item back at our cost, as the article "Returns" explains. After that, our warranty covers manufacturing defects; see the article "Warranty".

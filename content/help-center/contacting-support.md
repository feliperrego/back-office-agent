Our support assistant and our support team work together.

## What the assistant can do

The assistant answers questions from these help articles, quoting the article it used, and looks up the orders on your own account. It hands everything else to a member of our team: refunds, order changes, delivery problems, warranty claims and anything these articles don't cover. It only helps with Acme Outfitters orders and questions.

## Reaching our team

When the assistant hands your conversation over, a member of our team replies by email within 1 business day. You can also email us at support@acmeoutfitters.example.

## Your privacy

We discuss an order only with the customer who placed it. Neither the assistant nor our team shares anyone else's orders or account details.

---
title: Changing or canceling an order
category: Orders and payment
---

Plans change. What we can do depends on whether your order has shipped.

## Before your order ships

You can change the shipping address or the items in an order until your order ships. Ask our support team: the assistant passes your request to a member of the team, who makes the change and confirms it by email.

## After your order ships

Once an order has shipped, we can no longer change it. You can send the items back under our return policy, explained in the article "Returns".

## Canceling an order

Canceling follows the same rule: an order can be canceled until it ships. Ask our support team, and a member of the team will cancel it and confirm by email.
